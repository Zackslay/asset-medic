# Code of conduct

Asset Medic welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Help newcomers and allow room for honest mistakes.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam, or share anyone's private information.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project. Do not post malicious code or links intended to harm others.

These expectations apply to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and the project's support server.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the project maintainer, **Zackslay**, through the [Discord support server](https://discord.gg/4cxQwKtY3q). Ask to speak privately before sharing sensitive details. If Discord contact is unavailable, use GitHub's report-abuse tools for abusive content on GitHub.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports should be handled respectfully, with identifying details shared only as necessary to address the report.
